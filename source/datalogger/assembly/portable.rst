Portable datalogger
===================

External cables
---------------

External 12V power cable connected to Buccanneer PX0736/P.

.. list-table:: PX0736/P wiring table
    :widths: auto
    :header-rows: 1

    * - Pin No.
      - RockBLOCK+ connection
      - Colour
    * - L
      - 12V (live).
      - Brown or red (typical).
    * - N
      - 0V (neutral).
      - Blue or black (typical).

External Iridium modem connection for portable datalogger. Terminates into Bulgin Buccanneer PX0745/S.

.. list-table:: PX0745/P wiring table
    :widths: auto
    :header-rows: 1

    * - Pin No.
      - RockBLOCK+ connection
      - Colour
    * - 1
      - Iridium Ring Alert
      - Green
    * - 2
      - Iridium Network Available
      - White
    * - 3
      - On/off
      - Pink
    * - 4
      - RS-232 RX (output from RockBLOCK+)
      - Yellow
    * - 5
      - RS-232 RX (input to RockBLOCK+)
      - Blue
    * - 6
      - Supply voltage (9-30V)
      - Red
    * - 7
      - GND (0V)
      - Black
