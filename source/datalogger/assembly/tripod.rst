Tripod datalogger
=================
.. |times| unicode:: U+00D7

Datalogger parts
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
These should come as part of the datalogger supplied by APRS World.

* Sections of GRP pole: 3 |times| short 3 |times| long
* Central pelicase (yellow), with power system
* Joints: 3 |times| 'elbow' joints and central tri-directional joint
* 3 |times| steel feet (battery cases + ball-and-socket joint)
* 3 |times| batteries
* 2 |times| ratchet straps with hooks  
* bag of nuts and bolts + spares 
* Yagi antenna, with 2 \times{}RF cables
* 2 |times| solar panels
* solar panel mounting hardware

Other tools and equipment 
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* 1.85m length of cord (to measure length of ratchet straps during setup)
* 1/2 inch socket and wrench
* 9/16 inch socket and wrench
* Philips screwdriver
* drill + 10mm drill bit, if there is no mounting hole in the antenna
* self-amalgamating tape
* zip ties |times| 1 million
* rubber mallet

Assembly
~~~~~~~~~~~

The first time a datalogger is assembled, it can be quite difficult to get all of the pieces together (hence the rubber mallet). Once you have it assembled in a way that fits, it's worth labelling or colour-coding the pieces for next time.

#. Begin tripod assembly by inserting short GRP sections into the tri-directional joint, and bolting this to the base of the central pelicase
#. Complete the tripod assembly – attach legs to battery feet, then attach central star to legs. Some of the pieces might need to be encouraged with a mallet.
#. attach solar panels to two tripod legs, positioned to maximise sun exposure. Attach solar panel cables to labelled connectors on the bottom of the central pelicase. 
#. Attach ratchet straps between the battery feet, and adjust to 73” or 1.85m (use a piece of pre-measured cord to speed this up).
#. Assemble antenna: remember that the longest elements go nearest the top, the middle ones have the cable feed and the short ones go at the bottom. See antenna documentation. 
#. Attach antenna pointing downward underneath the central pelicase (drill mounting hole in antenna boom if necessary)
#. Connect antenna to RF inputs in the peli case, and then internally to the PCB using RF cables.
#. If leaving for long-term installation, seal all power connectors with self-amalgamating tape, and use zip ties to secure cables to the tripod legs. Leave some slack near the feet to allow them to flex without pulling on the cables.
